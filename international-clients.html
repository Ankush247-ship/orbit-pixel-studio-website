FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. Alongside businesses across Maharashtra, we work remotely with startups, freelancers and companies in other countries — the entire process, from discovery call to delivery, is designed to work over video call, WhatsApp and email.

What time zone do you work in, and how do calls work for international clients?

We're based in India (IST, UTC+5:30). For international clients we schedule discovery and review calls at a time that reasonably overlaps with your working hours, and keep everything else — updates, files and approvals — async over email and WhatsApp so time zone differences don't slow the project down.

How do international clients pay for a project?

We accept international bank transfer and other common online payment methods. Payment terms and currency are agreed upfront as part of the project proposal before work begins.

Why hire an India-based studio instead of a local agency?

Working with a studio in India typically means more competitive pricing for the same quality of website, SEO and marketing work, without compromising on communication — everything is documented in English, over email and WhatsApp, with clear timelines and regular updates.

What services can you deliver remotely?

The full range — website development, SEO, social media marketing, lead generation, branding and graphic design, video editing and AI-powered content — all delivered remotely with no on-site requirement.
