FAQ

Common Questions About Our Pricing.

What is the starting price for a website?

Website development starts at ₹9,999 per website. The Starter package, which bundles a business website with WhatsApp integration, basic SEO setup and mobile responsive design, starts at ₹7,999.

Which package should I choose?

Starter suits businesses beginning their digital journey with just a website. Growth (₹14,999+) is the most popular option and adds SEO, social media, lead generation and analytics. Custom is for businesses that need branding, AI automation, marketing and ads together.

Is ad spend included in the pricing?

No. Ad spend for Meta and Google campaigns is separate from the management fee. Prices listed are for our services; the advertising budget itself is set and paid by you directly to the ad platform.

Do prices change based on project requirements?

Yes. The prices shown are starting prices. Final pricing can vary depending on project scope, and custom packages are available for businesses with specific requirements.

How do I get started?

Reach out via WhatsApp, phone or the contact form and tell us about your business and goals. We'll recommend the right starting point, whether that's a single service or a full package.
