FAQ

Common Questions About Our Services.

Which service should I start with?

Most businesses start with website development, since that's the foundation everything else — SEO, ads, lead generation — points back to. If you already have a website, SEO or social media marketing is usually the next highest-impact step.

Can I combine multiple services?

Yes. Most clients combine a website with SEO or social media marketing rather than buying services one at a time. Our Growth and Custom packages on the pricing page bundle these at a lower combined cost — see pricing for details.

How long does a typical website take?

A standard business website usually takes 5–10 working days from content handover to launch, depending on scope. E-commerce or custom-designed sites take longer — we'll give you a specific timeline once we understand your requirements.

Do you work with businesses outside Ambernath and Kalyan?

Yes. While we're based in Ambernath and serve the wider Kalyan–Karjat corridor closely, we also work remotely with clients across India and internationally — see our international clients page for how remote projects work.

What's included in AI automation?

Our AI automation covers lead capture, AI-assisted content creation, and automated WhatsApp follow-up so enquiries get a response without manual work on your end. Scope depends on your existing tools and workflow — we'll map it out during a free consultation.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Yes. Maintenance plans cover content updates, performance optimization, uptime and security monitoring, and technical support after launch — see the Maintain service above or get in touch for a custom plan.

Every business is different — if your project needs a mix of services or a bespoke build, we'll scope it with you.
